How It Works

Booked, scanned, delivered

A simple three-step process designed around your schedule and your property manager's time.

01

Book a Visit

Send us the property address and a preferred scan time. We confirm within the hour and arrive on time with full equipment — no setup delays.

02

On-Site Scan

We scan every room using MagicPlan phone technology and a laser measure. A typical four-bedroom villa takes 45–60 minutes. No disruption to guests or owners.

03

Plan Production

Raw scan data is refined, labelled, dimensioned, and branded. We apply your agency logo, colour palette, and any specific formatting your portals require.

04

Delivery & Revisions

Files arrive by email as high-resolution PDF and PNG within your agreed turnaround window. One round of revisions included in every package.
